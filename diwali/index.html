Five Days of Diwali

Diwali is a festival of five days, each with its own meaning

1🪙

Dhanteras

Families clean their homes and buy something new, often gold, silver or utensils, to welcome prosperity.

2🪔

Naraka Chaturdashi

Also called Choti Diwali. It marks the victory of good over evil with early lamps and rangoli.

3🪷

Lakshmi Puja

The main night of Diwali. Homes glow with lamps and families pray to Lakshmi and Ganesha.

4⛰️

Govardhan Puja

Recalls Krishna lifting Govardhan hill. Food offerings are laid out, and in some regions it marks the new year.

5👫

Bhai Dooj

Sisters and brothers celebrate their bond with prayers, gifts and a shared meal.
