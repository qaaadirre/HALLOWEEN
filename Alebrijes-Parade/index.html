Plan Your Visit

📅

When

The parade is held once a year in October. Check the Museo de Arte Popular for this year’s date and time.

🗺️

Where

The route usually runs through the heart of Mexico City, from the Zócalo toward the Ángel de la Independencia.

🎒

Tips

Arrive early, wear comfy shoes, bring water and sun protection, and keep your phone ready for photos.

🖼️

After the parade

The creatures are often put on display afterwards, so you can admire the details up close.
